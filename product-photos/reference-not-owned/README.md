# Not your photos — do not upload these

I originally sorted these into a "ready to upload" folder based on filenames alone. I
was wrong to do that without looking at them first — I've now actually opened every one
and they're catalogue photography from someone else's website, not photos of anything
Kelly's Deli made. Evidence, so you can see for yourself:

- One shows a bagged snack with its own brand printed on it: **"KENT CRISPS"**, Union
  Jack logo, "LIMITED EDITION" badge (`loaded-crisps-ploughmans.PNG`).
- One shows deli pots with paper labels reading **"PICKY BITS DELI POT"** — a real,
  different deli's own product line (`pickt bits.PNG`).
- Every board photo carries a marketing badge that a business puts on its own website,
  not on a phone photo of finished food: "100% BRITISH CHARCUTERIE", "150/175 BITES ON
  EACH PLATTER", "BESTSELLER", "GLUTEN-FREE", "24 PORTIONS", "9 FILLINGS TO CHOOSE FROM",
  "VEGAN OPTIONS AVAILABLE", "BAKED FRESH EVERY MORNING", "NEW", "NEW LOOK",
  "4 INCREDIBLE FLAVOURS", "LIMITED EDITION".
- `ploughmans-board.PNG` has a left/right carousel arrow baked into the image — that's a
  screenshot of a website's product gallery.
- Several carry the same faint repeating watermark in the background.

If this is a supplier's catalogue you're sourcing boards from, or inspiration you saved
while researching what to make, that's completely fine to keep around for reference —
just not to put on kellysdeli.co.uk as if it's your own food. Nothing here was uploaded
to the live site.

What actually replaces this: real photos of the boards as your kitchen makes them.
