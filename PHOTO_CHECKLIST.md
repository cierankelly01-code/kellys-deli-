# Photo checklist

How this site actually works, so this list is useful: there's no folder to drop files
into. Log in at **kellysdeli.co.uk/admin → Menu & Pricing**, open a product, and upload
the photo there — the server resizes it automatically. A product only goes live once it
has a photo **and** a description **and** a price, and you tick it active — same as every
product on the site today. Nothing here goes live on its own; you flip the switch when
it's ready.

I've already sorted your loose photos from the repo root into two folders so nothing's a
mislabelled mystery file any more:

- **`product-photos/ready-to-upload/`** — photos I could confidently match to a specific
  product, renamed to say what they are. Upload these as-is.
- **`product-photos/needs-review/`** — photos I couldn't confidently match to anything on
  the list below. Tell me what each one is (or upload it yourself) and I'll wire it up.
